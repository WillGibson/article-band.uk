Article are super excited to be playing Graham's Stage at [Chalfest](https://chalfest.co.uk/) at 19:00 on Friday 16th July 2027.

[![Chalfest - Party on the Hill](images/chalfest-logo.png)](https://chalfest.co.uk/)

**Chalfest** - "Party on the Hill" is a community festival in Chalford, run by volunteers as a not-for-profit community interest company. It runs over Friday 16th and Saturday 17th July 2027.

The festival is at The France Lynch Pleasure Ground, Burcombe Way, Chalford, GL6 8LL. Check the [Chalfest info & map page](https://chalfest.co.uk/festival-info-2026/) for parking and transport.

Tickets are available from [Ticket Fairy](https://www.ticketfairy.com/event/chalfest2027-20260711110506514). They are valid for both days, and everyone needs one, including children.
